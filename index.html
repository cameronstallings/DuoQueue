DuoQueue

DuoQueue is an 18+ app for finding people to play games with. It's platonic, not dating. You're matching on games, schedules, and vibe so you always have someone good to duo with.

Swipe on profiles built around what you play, when you play, and how you like to play it, then chat and queue up with the people you match.
